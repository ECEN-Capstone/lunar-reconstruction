# Negative-control review: pose acceptance is not reconstruction validity

2026-11-09. All runs use existing pipeline modules without edits. No learned model
or Jarvis was used. These are deliberately constructed diagnostic videos, not
results from the colleague's GPU.

## Refreshed positive control

The original 480-pixel procedural video was copied from temporary storage into
`../v2_control_2026-11-09/control480.mp4` after matching its recorded SHA256. The
current pose checker and evaluator were rerun against the corrected schema-2
pose-anchor bundle. All 33 poses and 24 unconditioned frames were accepted.
The terrain surfaces, reconstructed trajectory and validation arrays reproduce
the historical control exactly, element for element. Pose checking took 16.0 s
and evaluation 9.1 s on this PC.

The result remains 638 candidate video cells, 21/561 scored reference cells and
4.90 cm video RMSE versus 0.85 cm same-cell linear RMSE. It is a reproducibility
control, not an accuracy success. This refresh repairs the old bundle-version
gap without changing the historical data or pretending that the control was
generated by VACE. The full command log and hashes are in
`../v2_control_2026-11-09/review_run.json`.

## Deliberately invalid temporal constructions

Both clips are 33 frames at 480 pixels, made from the real endpoint images in the
schema-2 endpoints-only bundle. A frozen clip repeats the first image, including
at the final frame; a crossfade linearly blends the endpoints and preserves both
ends. The source construction is recorded separately from the evaluator's broad
`procedural_control` label. MP4 compression can introduce small frame differences.

| Diagnostic | Accepted unconditioned poses | Sparse triangulated points | Candidate video cells | Scored withheld cells |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Frozen first endpoint | 31 / 31 | 0 | 4 | 0 / 561 |
| Linear endpoint crossfade | 24 / 31 | 292 | 49 | 1 / 561 |

Both evaluator commands returned success and exported candidate meshes. Their
status remains experimental; neither qualifies as validated terrain.

The frozen clip has an estimated camera-position range of about 1.69 cm along
the largest coordinate axis, despite no camera motion in its construction. No
sparse tracking window passes the baseline check. Its four candidate terrain
cells therefore arise through the dense route. None has withheld reference
support. Raw-before-envelope error is 50.05 cm over four other scored raw cells;
these are not the four final accepted cells.

The crossfade poses remain near one endpoint and then the other, with a 0.887 m
change between successive accepted poses across an eight-frame index gap. It
does not recover a continuously evidenced physical traversal. Yet global endpoint
displacement alone would appear sufficient. Its single scored candidate cell has
0.858 cm error versus 0.538 cm linear interpolation. One cell cannot establish
terrain accuracy. Raw-before-envelope RMSE is 85.66 cm over 28 cells; those are a
different subset from the final scored cell.

## Implications for Astra's implementation

1. `video_pose_check` is an anchor-conditioned camera diagnostic, not a detector
   of rigid-scene validity. A stationary camera can legitimately have accepted
   poses. Report accepted-frame counts separately from triangulation support.
2. `video_triangulate.sweep` currently lacks a geometric parallax/baseline test
   for each accepted height cell. Add a support test based on the actual contributing
   output camera poses/rays. A whole-video baseline threshold is insufficient for
   a crossfade and a per-frame count is insufficient for repeated near-identical views.
   Retain photometric ambiguity and reprojection checks as well. Keep thresholds
   explicit and test them on the positive control rather than merely tuning them
   to eliminate these two examples.
3. Preserve original temporal indices when reasoning about gaps and tracks. The
   current evaluator compresses accepted frames into a shorter list. A sequence
   with an eight-frame gap must not be described as continuously tracked motion.
   A large jump alone is not proof of an invalid trajectory, but is a diagnostic
   needing multi-view evidence rather than silent interpolation.
4. A process exit code, nonempty OBJ, accepted PnP frames or tiny error on one cell
   cannot be the promotion criterion. Require adequate scored coverage, independent
   multi-view support, and improvement over fixed-region interpolation. There is
   no new universal coverage threshold established by these two fixtures.
5. Add these clips as negative regression fixtures when modifying the evaluator.
   The frozen clip should yield no useful new terrain; the crossfade must not be
   promoted as a validated spatial bridge. A candidate-generation stage may still
   save rejected diagnostics, as long as downstream acceptance records the failure.

No implementation patch is applied here because Astra owns that code. The earlier
review requests about duplicate seeds, consistent experiment settings and hash
binding remain relevant. This evidence narrows the acceptance problem before the
learned experiment is judged.
